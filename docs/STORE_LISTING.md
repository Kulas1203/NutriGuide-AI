## Full description (draft)

NutriGuide AI helps you eat well with personalized meal plans, easy food
tracking, gentle fasting tools, and an AI Nutrition Coach that explains the
"why" with sources — always in plain, supportive language.

What you can do:
- Discover and compare evidence-based eating styles (balanced, Mediterranean,
  high-protein, low-carb, vegetarian, vegan, DASH, and more) — no diet is
  pushed as "the one".
- Get transparent calorie and macro estimates you can adjust, with the formula
  shown and a built-in safety minimum.
- Generate weekly meal plans tailored to your diet, budget, cooking time and
  allergies, with grocery lists and easy swaps.
- Log food fast, with clear labels for verified vs. estimated nutrition.
- Track water, gentle intermittent fasting (12:12–16:8), weight trends and
  small daily habits.
- Ask the AI Nutrition Coach general nutrition questions and get answers with
  sources, a confidence indicator, and a nudge to see a professional when a
  question goes beyond general wellness.

Your privacy matters: your data is yours. Export it or delete your account
anytime. We never sell health data or use it for ads, and we don't train AI
models on your private conversations without your explicit opt-in.

Important: NutriGuide AI provides educational nutrition information,
meal-planning assistance, food tracking, and general wellness guidance. It does
not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical conditions and is not a
substitute for advice from a physician or registered dietitian. It is designed
for adults (18+).
