# Wellness & Medical Disclaimer

> DRAFT — Review by qualified professionals required before publication.

NutriGuide AI provides educational nutrition information, meal-planning assistance, food tracking, and general wellness guidance. **It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent medical conditions and is not a substitute for advice from a physician or registered dietitian.**

## The AI is not a clinician

The AI Nutrition Coach is not a doctor and not a registered dietitian. It offers general educational information, is not always correct, and can make mistakes. It will not diagnose conditions, interpret symptoms, prescribe medication, or promise health outcomes.

## When to seek professional care

Please consult a qualified professional, and do not rely on this app, if you:

- are pregnant or breastfeeding;
- have or may have an eating disorder;
- have diabetes requiring medication, kidney, liver, or cardiovascular disease, cancer, or another serious condition;
- take medicines affected by food intake;
- experience fainting, severe weakness, confusion, chest pain, or other urgent symptoms.

## Emergencies

If you may be experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services or seek immediate medical care. Do not wait for information from this app.

## Estimates

Calorie, macronutrient, and recipe values are estimates and vary by person and preparation. Values labeled as estimates are not laboratory-measured.
