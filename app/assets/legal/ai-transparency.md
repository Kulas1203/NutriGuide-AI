# AI Transparency Notice

> DRAFT — Review required before publication.

## How the AI Coach works

When you ask the AI Nutrition Coach a question, your message is sent over an encrypted connection to our secure backend. The backend adds evidence-grounded context (for example USDA nutrition data and reviewed educational content), applies a safety layer, and requests an answer from an AI provider. The provider's credentials never touch your device.

## What the AI can and cannot do

- It gives general educational nutrition information with sources and a confidence indicator.
- It distinguishes established evidence from general guidance and uncertain evidence, and it will say when it does not know.
- It does **not** diagnose, prescribe, promise outcomes, or replace a professional.
- It does **not** invent studies, citations, nutrient values, or credentials.

## Safety layer

A server-side safety layer detects urgent, eating-disorder, dangerous-fasting, severe-restriction, child-dieting, pregnancy, medication, and serious-disease situations. In those cases the app responds with appropriate guidance and professional-referral messaging rather than a detailed diet answer.

## Your data and model training

We do not train AI models on your private conversations without your separate, explicit, informed opt-in consent. You can disable AI conversation history at any time and keep using the app.

## Model configuration

The specific model version is configured securely on the backend and can change as safer or better models become available. New models and prompts are evaluated against our safety evaluation suite before release.
