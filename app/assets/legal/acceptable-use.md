# Community & Acceptable Use Policy

> DRAFT — Review required before publication.

To keep NutriGuide AI safe and useful, you agree not to:

- use the app if you are under 18;
- attempt to bypass or disable safety features;
- use the AI Coach to obtain instructions for self-harm, disordered eating, or dangerous fasting;
- submit content that is illegal, abusive, or violates others' rights;
- attempt to extract secrets, attack the backend, or abuse rate limits;
- misrepresent the app's guidance as medical advice from a licensed professional.

We may limit or suspend access where necessary to protect users or the service. If you are struggling, please reach out to a qualified professional or a crisis line in your country.
