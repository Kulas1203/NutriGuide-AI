## Summary

NutriGuide AI treats your diet, body-measurement, allergy, conversation and wellness information as sensitive personal data. We collect only what a feature needs, never sell your data, and never use your health data for advertising.

## Information we collect

- **Account data:** email address and authentication identifiers (via Firebase Authentication).
- **Profile data you provide:** preferred name, age, height, weight, activity level, goals, diet preference, allergies, foods to avoid, and optional health considerations you choose to share.
- **Usage data you create:** food-diary entries, meal plans, fasting sessions, water logs, weight/waist entries, habits, and AI Coach conversations (only if you keep AI history enabled).
- **Diagnostics:** crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics) with health information excluded, and aggregate, non-identifying usage metrics.

## How we use information

- To personalize meal plans, nutrition targets, and educational guidance.
- To operate the AI Nutrition Coach through our secure backend.
- To keep the service reliable and secure (abuse prevention, rate limiting).

We do **not** use your personal or health information for advertising, and we do **not** enable personalized advertising.

## AI conversations

AI Coach messages are processed by our backend and an AI provider solely to generate your answer. We do **not** train AI models on your private conversations without your separate, explicit, informed opt-in. You can disable AI conversation history at any time and still use the rest of the app.

## Sharing

We share data only with infrastructure processors that operate the service (for example {{CLOUD_PROVIDER}} and the configured AI provider), under contract, for the purposes above. See DATA_SAFETY_MAPPING.md for the full list.

## Your controls

- Export your data at any time (Settings → Data export).
- Delete your account and associated data at any time (Settings → Delete account).
- Disable AI conversation history (Settings → AI data controls).
- Use private notifications so no health detail appears on your lock screen.

## Data retention

We retain your data until you delete it. Deleted accounts are removed from active systems promptly; see the Data Retention Policy for backup timelines.

## Children

NutriGuide AI is for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children.
