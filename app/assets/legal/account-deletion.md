# Account Deletion Instructions

You can delete your NutriGuide AI account and all associated data at any time.

## In the app

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Confirm and re-enter your password to verify it is you.
4. Your local data is wiped and a server-side deletion of your account and data is triggered.

## What is deleted

Your profile, food diary, meal plans, fasting history, progress entries, habits, and AI conversation history.

## Without the app

If you cannot access the app, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from your registered email address with the subject "Delete my account" and we will process the deletion and confirm by reply.
